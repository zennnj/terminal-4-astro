Terminal 4 is a personal archive for articles, memos, artwork, and reviews.

## Why rebuild it

The old site used Hexo. As the archive grew beyond articles, it was migrated to Astro and reorganized from Astro Yi as a starting point.

## Current structure

- **ARTICLE**  
  Long-form writing and technical notes
- **MEMOS**  
  Short thoughts recorded anytime
- **GALLERY**  
  Original characters and fan art
- **REVIEW**  
  Notes and final impressions

## Technology & credits

Built with Astro. The first structure started from the open-source [Astro Yi](https://github.com/cirry/astro-yi) theme.
